# Track A1 (M3): grid, filters and peek

Follow [M3-wave-A.md](M3-wave-A.md) and [README.md](README.md).

**You own:** `features/{grid,filters,peek}/**` and the per-track files named in M3-wave-A.md.

**Starting point:** the M2 slice already has the virtualised justified/square grid, tiles, selection, and
detail open and close. Extend it; don't rebuild it.

**Deliverables** (interaction-spec §2–§4, screens.md, the prototype's `grid.js`, `toolbar.js`, `peek.js`):

1. **Tiles:**
   - badges: Alt missing, video/audio duration, PDF page count;
   - non-image cards with a type icon;
   - SVG and PNG on a checkerboard;
   - upload and processing state rendered natively from the tile upload-state store (B2 feeds it in
     M4; test it with the store now);
   - the "just changed" flash;
   - the hover file name and check circle.
2. **Selection:**
   - the full pointer and keyboard behaviour from interaction-spec §2, including ↑/↓ to the nearest
     centre in the row above or below, Home/End, Shift ranges, Ctrl/Cmd+A, X and Esc;
   - the **selection bar** at 2 or more selected: "✕ N selected · Move to folder · Edit details ·
     Download · Delete". Its actions call commands that other tracks implement; until then they show as
     disabled. Under D33 it says "N hidden by filters" when some selected items are hidden;
   - selected tiles scale with the ring and check.
3. **Filters and sort** (the filter bar):
   - search with a 150 ms debounce;
   - type chips with counts, hidden at 0;
   - the Missing alt chip with its count;
   - the "+ Filter" menu: size, orientation, and months from the facets;
   - removable chips for active filters;
   - the sort menu;
   - the live summary (`N items · total size`).

   All of it lives in the URL (D16 replace rules).
4. **Density and layout:** a slider and `+`/`-`, the segmented control and `L`. They keep the focused
   tile anchored (spike C1).
5. **States:**
   - skeleton rows shaped like the justified grid;
   - "This folder is empty" (with an upload action stub);
   - "No media matches" with Clear filters;
   - the empty library;
   - no spinners in content areas.
6. **Peek (`Space`):** the Quick Look overlay. Arrows keep moving grid focus and the peek follows;
   Space or Esc closes it; Enter opens detail. The grid, selection and URL don't change.
7. **Performance:** meet plan §9 at 50k in mock mode (`?mock-scenario=large`): no long tasks while
   scrolling, layout ≤ 50 ms. Measure and report.

**Contract notes:** the facets scopes (`view` for the summary, `folder` for chips and months) are in
server-api.md §7.4. Use F2's hooks.

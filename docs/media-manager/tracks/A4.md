# Track A4 (M3): inspector

Follow [M3-wave-A.md](M3-wave-A.md) and [README.md](README.md).

**You own:** `features/inspector/**` (it hosts the `EditPanel` stub that B1 fills in M4), and the
per-track files named in M3-wave-A.md, including `core/data/inspector.ts`.

**Starting point:** the M2 slice has the rail plus a lazy panel, the single-item Title and Alt autosave
with optimistic rollback, drafts keyed `id:field`, Retry, the read-only Author state, `A`, the logout
reset, and D52 (the panel follows the stage in detail mode). Extend it.

**Deliverables** ([specs/inspector.md](../design/specs/inspector.md) §1–§5 and §7–§9, screens.md):

1. **The rail:** Info, Edit image (the stub panel for B1) and Usage. Clicking the active icon collapses
   the panel; `I` toggles it; `E` opens Edit. It carries badges: a warn dot for missing alt, a usage
   count, and an accent dot while edits are unsaved.
2. **Info for 0 items:**
   - the folder summary: items, size, missing alt (a button that turns on the filter), unused;
   - an alt-coverage meter with "Start alt-text pass";
   - a type breakdown;
   - "You can edit N of M".
3. **Info for 1 item:**
   - all fields: Title, Alt (image/SVG only), Caption, Description;
   - the collapsible File, Camera (only with EXIF) and Sizes sections, with their open state kept per
     session;
   - Copy URL;
   - the actions Edit image, Download and Delete, as commands (some show as disabled until B1 and B3);
   - the Preview in grid mode only.
4. **Info for N items (batch):**
   - a strip of up to 6 thumbnails, and "Editing N items";
   - Mixed fields with a dashed edge, where committing an empty Mixed field is a no-op;
   - Description with Replace | Append;
   - alt text only on image-like items;
   - commits through the bulk batch (D24, client-chunked, with progress), and an Undo toast with the
     per-item `previous` values;
   - D33: say when the selection includes hidden items.
5. **Save & next (the alt-text pass):**
   - `Enter` in Title or Alt saves and moves to the next item in collection order, keeping the field and
     caret; `Shift+Enter` goes back;
   - the progress strip;
   - Start pass turns on the Missing alt filter and focuses the first image's alt. Saved items drop out
     of the filtered view; the cursor must already have moved on (the M2 `stepFrom` helper);
   - when the last one is saved, the filter turns off and a completion toast celebrates once per run;
   - works in grid and in detail mode (D52).
6. **The Usage panel:**
   - "Used in N places" with per-post contexts and edit links;
   - the featured-image warning;
   - the site-level refs, including the Visual Portfolio placeholder (D44: `viewUrl` null);
   - attach and detach with a post picker (published posts only, D21) and an Undo toast;
   - batch aggregates;
   - the "index may be incomplete" footnote.
7. **Read-only and permissions:** mixed batches apply to the user's own items only, with an explanation.
   Fields are `readonly` rather than `disabled`.
8. **The known M2 gap:** the panel's "Unsaved changes" note must also count typing done during a save.

**Backend:** usage, the batch route and post search exist (server-api.md §7.6, §7.9, §7.14). Mock
scenarios exist in F2's fake; extend `mocks/handlers/inspector.ts` as needed.

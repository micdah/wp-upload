# Track A2 (M3): detail mode

Follow [M3-wave-A.md](M3-wave-A.md) and [README.md](README.md).

**You own:** `features/detail/**` and the per-track files named in M3-wave-A.md.

**Starting point:** the M2 slice has detail as a lazy overlay, with the morph (spike C2's rules), history
(push on enter, replace on step), stepping that stops at the ends (D34), the stage's sub-size upgrade and
neighbour preload, fit/100 % zoom, Download, the return ring, and D52 (the inspector follows the stage).
Extend it.

**Deliverables** (interaction-spec §5, screens.md, the prototype's `detail.js`, `transition.js`):

1. **Stage per media type:**
   - **video:** poster, play, time, scrubber, duration;
   - **audio:** a card with title, duration and player bar;
   - **PDF:** the first-page preview as a paper sheet, plus "page 1 of N", Open PDF and Download;
   - **archive and other:** a card with Download;
   - **SVG:** on a checkerboard.

   Each player control is keyboard-reachable and doesn't fight the global keymap (use `useKeyLayer` and
   `[data-mm-local-keys]` per spike C4's rules).
2. **Virtual filmstrip** (TanStack Virtual):
   - a strip of the collection, dimmed except the current item, which has a neutral outline and stays
     centred;
   - selected items keep their ring and check;
   - Ctrl/Cmd+click toggles selection from the strip;
   - clicking an item steps there (replace);
   - mark it `data-mm-no-select` (iPad lesson).
3. **Zoom and pan:**
   - `Z` toggles Fit ↔ 100 %, and `+`/`-` step Fit → 50 → 100 → 200 %;
   - when zoomed, drag to pan with the grab cursor, and trackpad pan;
   - zoom resets to Fit when you step or leave;
   - on touch, pinch-zoom on the stage image if feasible.
4. **The top bar:** Back, title, index (`12 / 93`, mono), Zoom, Download and **More**: Copy URL, Open in
   wp-admin, Move to folder, Edit image, Delete. Move, Edit and Delete call commands that later tracks
   implement; until then they show as disabled.
5. **Swipe to step on touch** (system.md §5): commits past 64 px or on a flick, with resistance at the
   ends.
6. **Edge cases:**
   - a deep link to an item not in the current collection (stepping walks from its last known
     position);
   - items removed while open;
   - reduced motion (cross-fade).

**Coordination:** the filmstrip reads the collection through `useCollection()`. The tile upload state may
appear on filmstrip items too.
